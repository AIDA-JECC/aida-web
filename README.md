## 📌 About the Department

The **Department of Artificial Intelligence and Data Science (AIDA)** at **Jyothi Engineering College (JECC)** was established in 2022 to spearhead technological innovation, cutting-edge research, and industry-aligned education in the fields of AI, Machine Learning, and Big Data Engineering.

The department and its official student association (**AIDA**) bring together student innovators, researchers, and tech enthusiasts to solve complex real-world challenges through data-driven solutions, ethical AI governance, and collaborative engineering.

---

## 🎯 Vision & Mission

### 👁️ Department Vision
> *"To create ethical leaders in the domain of Artificial Intelligence and Data Science through an effectual teaching and learning process, developing emerging technology solutions for the benefit of industry and society with a focus on holistic learning and excellence."*

### 🚀 Department Mission
1. **Academic Excellence & Infrastructure**: Provide state-of-the-art academic and practical GPU-accelerated computing infrastructure to foster mastery in core Computer Science and cutting-edge AI technologies.
2. **Research & Innovation**: Cultivate an innovation-driven research mindset, encouraging students to solve complex real-world data engineering challenges.
3. **Ethical Leadership**: Instill high ethical standards, collaborative leadership, and societal commitment in every engineering solution.
4. **Industry-Academia Ecosystem**: Establish strong industry-academia bridges through hands-on hackathons, technical symposiums, and expert mentorship.
